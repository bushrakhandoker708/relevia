Things to try when it's a lot

Small exercises you can do anywhere. Pick whichever fits how much energy you have.

  • Breathing in fours 2 min
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding 3 min
  • Tense and release 8 min
  • A calm place 5 min
  • Writing it out any time
  • A short walk 10 min
  • Kind sentences 1 min
for when your chest feels tight

Breathing in fours

About two minutes. Sitting or lying down.

  1. Breathe in through your nose for a count of four.
  2. Hold gently for four. Don't force it.
  3. Breathe out slowly for four.
  4. Repeat four or five times, then notice how your shoulders feel.
